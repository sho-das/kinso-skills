# Stage 4 - Blog and launch article

Read `../RULES-RESEARCH.md` and `BRAIN.md` first. Paths are from `kinso-promote/`. Optional on the Any launch way (for example a local shop), when you say so at the start.

## Runs

Each skill is followed as its own `SKILL.md` says, read in place.

1. `skills/article-writing/SKILL.md`: voice from your samples, its banned patterns and quality gate.
2. `skills/draft-content/SKILL.md` (blog post section): format by channel. Its connectors (`skills/kw-shared/CONNECTORS.md`) are not used.
3. `skills/claude-seo-seo-content/SKILL.md`: SEO title, description and heading structure only.
4. `skills/content-strategy/SKILL.md`: 2 to 4 later post titles.

Skip: `scripts/claude-seo` commands in seo-content (including its humanize script: humanizer runs in its place); content-strategy's headless CMS part.

## In

- `promote/MESSAGE.md`, `promote/FACTS.md`, `promote/MUST-NOT.md`, `promote/GAPS.md`, `handoff/PROMOTE-BUILD.md`, voice samples from `START.md`.
- `promote/AUDIENCE.md` (stage 1): the blog and newsletter rows (length, keyword approach). If you did not choose blog or newsletter, write only the SEO title and description, or skip the stage when you say so.

## Deal and pick

- **Deal 3 openings** (first 3 to 5 sentences): A a real story or moment; B the problem in numbers, only numbers in `FACTS.md`; C the demo moment (what the reader sees the product do).
- Openings A and B come from article-writing and draft-content in turn; the seed `<job>-promote-04` decides which skill writes which (draw 1 of 2, written in the pick). C goes to the other skill.
- If `FACTS.md` holds no number, B is "problem in plain words" and says so.
- Before the openings: the writer, the voice card and the scene bank from `BRAIN.md` "Writing that sounds human" (steps 1 to 3). Opening A uses a scene from the bank (a place scene, or a reader scene when no real story may be told). Each opening names its scene number.
- **Rubric (0 to 3 per line):** opens with a real moment or fact, no warm-up; one main point; every section adds something new; ends with a real next step; true to `FACTS.md`; sounds like the voice card; rival test.
- A fresh judge scores the openings with skill names hidden. The winner is drafted in full by the skill that wrote it. Save the others in `promote/ALTERNATIVES.md`. Write `<job>/picks/promote/04-PICK.md`.

## Guards

- Shared guards from `BRAIN.md` (never post; never main accounts; no MUST-NOT claim; no invented numbers, quotes, users or testimonials; platform rules; free only; local tools only).
- No story about a real person unless it is in `FACTS.md` and approved (a founder's or family story that is "not decided" stays out).
- No quotes unless they passed the collector's `quote-check` and have consent.
- A call to action that is not live is a marked slot.
- Never publish to a CMS or blog host.

## Checks after

- Every claim, number and date traces to a `FACTS.md` row. Numbers match their source exactly.
- MUST-NOT scan on the title, description and body.
- SEO title and description lengths checked against a dated source; numbers inside skill files are not sources.
- Links real or marked slots.
- **Shape:** the body follows one scene or point through (`BRAIN.md` step 7). Hours and booking sit in one short block near the end, never as their own headings. No heading names a notice-board topic ("Horários", "Como reservar", "Hours"). Sections are uneven in length. A post under 400 words has at most one subheading. The practical block is written in the voice, as one or two sentences, never a semicolon list of hours. No sample message or line of dialogue is written for the reader to copy, and no line only sets up the next one (humanizer section 4).
- **Useful to act on** (`BRAIN.md` section of that name): the first paragraph answers the main keyword's question (what, where, when, how to book) inside the scene; the post gives the searcher one thing the bio does not, described from `FACTS.md` rows only (for example how a booking goes, from the message to the table); the body never ends on a fact list.
- **Rhythm and repetition:** `BRAIN.md` steps 5 and 6. No line about the text itself. The bio line at most once.
- Humanizer pass with the full list (`BRAIN.md` step 8, table in `promote/HUMANIZER.md`) and, for mixed readers, clear-English pass. They remove tells only and keep the voice, the facts and the structure.
- **Sounds human** 7 or more from a fresh judge (`BRAIN.md`). The runner writes "pending fresh judge" if it cannot start one.
- No em dashes.
