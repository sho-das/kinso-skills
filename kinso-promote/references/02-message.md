## Deal and pick

- **Deal 3 angles**, one per skill above. Never blend them into one.
- **Seeded draw for the council seats.** Seed `<job>-promote-02`. Menu: the "Positioning / messaging" and "Launch strategy" rows of the council's seating table. Draw 2 strong fits, then 1 dissenter from the same rows. Write the seed and the 3 names.
- Each angle is: one line; the buyer (from `PROMOTE.md`); the one edge; 3 proof points from `FACTS.md`, each with its row number; what can be shown on screen.
- **Rubric (0 to 3 per line):** true to `FACTS.md`; names the buyer; uses the one edge; rival test (put the nearest rival's name in, and if the line still fits, score 0); can be shown, not only said; fits your voice samples (or plain, if none).
- Reasons decide, not the total. A fresh judge scores with the skill names hidden.
- Keep 1. Save the other 2 in `promote/ALTERNATIVES.md`. Write `<job>/picks/promote/02-PICK.md`.

## Guards

- Shared guards from `BRAIN.md` (never post; never main accounts; no MUST-NOT claim; no invented numbers, quotes, users or testimonials; platform rules; free only; local tools only).
- The council is a simulation. No advisor's name, view or quote goes into any public text. Its grounding rules stand.
- Psychology angles that turn into a claim (fear, scarcity, "only N left") are dropped unless the fact is in `FACTS.md`.
- No price, free tier or trial in the angle when `GAPS.md` says price is not decided.

## Checks after

- Every proof point traces to a `FACTS.md` row.
- MUST-NOT scan on all 3 angles and on `CONTEXT.md`.
- Humanizer pass and, for mixed readers, clear-English pass on `MESSAGE.md`. These remove tells only. They never flatten the voice of a sample-based line.
- No em dashes.

## Override

You can pick a different angle, or write your own. The brain shows its reason once, plus the strongest case for your choice, then follows you and adds a row to `OVERRIDES.md`.

## Out

- `promote/MESSAGE.md`: the chosen angle, one-line pitch, 3 proof points with `FACTS.md` rows, voice notes ("from samples" or "not from samples").
- `promote/CONTEXT.md` (from product-marketing), `promote/ALTERNATIVES.md`, `<job>/picks/promote/02-PICK.md`.
