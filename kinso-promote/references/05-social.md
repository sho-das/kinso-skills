# Stage 5 - Social posts per platform

Read `../RULES-RESEARCH.md` and `BRAIN.md` first. Paths are from `kinso-promote/`.

## Runs

Each skill is followed as its own `SKILL.md` says, read in place.

1. `skills/social/SKILL.md` with `references/platforms.md`, `platform-limits.md`, `post-templates.md`, `carousel-frameworks.md`: hook families, platform notes, carousels.
2. `skills/content-engine/SKILL.md`: one claim per post, its X and LinkedIn rules, hard bans.
3. `skills/crosspost/SKILL.md`: the adapt rules only (voice fingerprint, adapt by platform, never identical copy).

Skip: social's scheduling, engagement routine and listening parts; crosspost's posting order; any tool registry or API. Log the skips in `promote/PROCESS.md`.

## In

- `promote/MESSAGE.md`, `promote/FACTS.md`, `promote/MUST-NOT.md`, `promote/GAPS.md`, platforms, language and voice samples from `START.md`, the blog draft from stage 4 if any.
- `promote/AUDIENCE.md` (stage 1): per platform, the format, length, hashtag and keyword approach, and posts per week. Only the platforms and formats you chose are written.

## Deal and pick

- **Per post, deal 3 hooks from 3 different hook families.** Menu: social's hook families (curiosity, story, value, contrarian, social proof). Social proof is in the menu only if `FACTS.md` holds a real proof row.
- **Seeded draw.** Seed `<job>-promote-05-<post id>` (for example `kinbeat-promote-05-li-2`). Draw 3 distinct families.
- **Rotation rule:** a family that opened the last post on the same platform is taken out of the menu before the draw. No two posts in a row open the same way.
- Before the draws: the writer, the voice card and the scene bank from `BRAIN.md` "Writing that sounds human" (steps 1 to 3). Each post leads with its own scene from the bank (scene number written). One image or line (for example the stove) appears in at most 2 posts of the whole set, slides included.
- Each hook is filled with one fact from `FACTS.md` (row number written) and one scene. A hook that works only as a formula scores 0. A greeting formula used as the opening line ("Oi, gente!") counts as a formula.
- social and content-engine each draft the platform version from the winning hook. The brain keeps the better draft. It never merges the two into one voice.
- **Rubric (0 to 3 per line):** one real claim; the hook works because of a specific fact; native to the platform; sounds like your samples (or plain if none); any call to action is real and approved; no engagement bait; rival test.
- Losing hooks go to `promote/ALTERNATIVES.md`. Write `<job>/picks/promote/05-PICK.md` (one table per post).

## Guards

- Shared guards from `BRAIN.md` (never post, like, message or schedule; never main accounts; no MUST-NOT claim; no invented numbers, quotes, users or testimonials; platform rules; free only; local tools only).
- Platform rules: limits and community rules read from each platform's own help page, with a date. A rule not read is marked "unread". `platform-limits.md` in the skill is a starting point, never the source.
- No rival's text, hooks or format copied. Rivals are named only as `FACTS.md` allows.
- No tags of real people or accounts unless you list them in `START.md`.

## Checks after

- Every claim traces to a `FACTS.md` row; numbers match.
- MUST-NOT scan on every post, carousel slide and caption.
- Character limits per platform, from the dated help page: each post and each slide counted, with the count written next to it. Hashtag limits the same way.
- No identical text across platforms (crosspost rule).
- Alt text written for every image slot.
- **Carousel is one scene told across slides,** not one fact per card. The slides follow the lead scene in order (before, during, after) in the voice card's words; the hours and the booking sit together on at most one slide. A slide that only states a fact is allowed once (r3 judge: one-fact cards scored 5). **Fact-only** means every word on the slide is a `FACTS.md` row or a label for one: name, area, handle, days, hours, closed day, booking channel. A name-and-handle end card ("Casa da Vó / Santa Tereza, BH / @casadavobh") is fact-only and counts. The bio line alone on a slide also counts. Count them per carousel and write the count and the slide numbers in the file; the writer's own "slide N is the one fact-only slide" is not the count (r4: slides 4 and 6 were both fact-only, `tests/new/r4/stage9-final-edit/C/JUDGE-R4.md:13`). With 2 or more, merge the facts onto one slide (no fact added or dropped) or give the extra slide a line of the lead scene.
- **Status and short posts read like a message to someone you know:** one scene, one ask at the end. No two notice lines in a row ("A reserva é pelo WhatsApp. Não tem pagamento online."). No hook built from 3 staged beats.
- **Useful to act on** (`BRAIN.md` section of that name): each post names a reason to come; no cover, first slide or first line leads with a closed day or a "no"; the one ask names the action, the channel and the day or meal ("combinar" alone fails); each post alone gives what, when and how to book; "no online payment" in at most one post of the set.
- **Rhythm and repetition:** `BRAIN.md` steps 5 and 6, per caption and across the set. Carousel slides do not all use the same sentence shape. No two captions open or close the same way.
- Humanizer pass with the full list (`BRAIN.md` step 8, table in `promote/HUMANIZER.md`); clear-English pass for mixed readers. These remove tells only. They never flatten the voice, the line breaks you chose, or the emoji in your samples.
- **Sounds human** 7 or more per post from a fresh judge (`BRAIN.md`). The runner writes "pending fresh judge" if it cannot start one.
- No em dashes. Non-English posts: marked "a native speaker checks before posting".
