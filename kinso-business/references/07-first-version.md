## Deal and pick

Deal **3 first-version scopes**:
- **Small** - no product: a test that earns money or a commitment (invoice test, manual service, pre-order).
- **Ideal** - the smallest product: one-sentence MVP, 3 to 6 features, 5 steps at most.
- **Sideways** - seeded draw from: a fake door page (`handoff/TEST-PAGE.md`), a narrow service for one segment, a concierge version, a plug-in on an existing platform. Excludes the 2 above.

Rubric, 0-3 per row, max 18: tests the riskiest assumption (with a kill condition); earns money or a commitment; fits the stage 3 budget and the founders' time; inside the guards (no red or orange stage 6 item in scope); small; one metric with a target and a date.

Then the MomTest verdict on the winner: build, build as experiment, or run N conversations first. It never blocks. Write `<job>/picks/business/07-PICK.md`.

- Never invent users, quotes or test results. Build times are for these founders, not an agency.
