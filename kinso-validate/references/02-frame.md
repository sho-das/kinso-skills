# Stage 2 - Frame

Read `../RULES-RESEARCH.md` and `BRAIN.md` first. Paths are from `kinso-validate/`. Old rewrite: `references/rewrite/02-frame.md`.

## Runs

1. `skills/startup-design/SKILL.md` intake (the stage winner) with `references/intake-questions.md` and `references/honesty-protocol.md`: 3 to 5 questions at a time, probe vague answers, summarise after 2 to 3 rounds.
2. `skills/validate-idea/SKILL.md` (with `skills/shared/conventions.md`): the five fatal questions, as assumptions.
3. `skills/identify-assumptions-new/SKILL.md`: 8 risk categories, including adoption.
4. `skills/idea-refine/refinement-criteria.md`: Must, Should, Might be true; riskiest assumption first.
5. `skills/office-hours/sections/phase-2a-startup-diagnostic.md`: routing by product stage only.

Skip: the startup-design pre-flight searches (they run at the start of stage 4). startup-design research waves (stage 3). office-hours preamble and `bin/`.

## In

- `idea/BRAINSTORM.md` and the founder's pick, or the clear idea (Validate way in), and `START.md`.
- Shared rules: facts only, free only, never a `-contributor` model, privacy, user override. No web.

## Deal and pick

- Questions are routed, not random: by product stage, then only the ones not yet answered.
- Deal 3 framings of the one-line idea: A by the narrowest user, B by the payer, C by the job.
- Rubric lines (0 to 3): each assumption falsifiable; has pass and fail numbers; cheapest check fits the founder's time and money; covers a fatal question.
- Keep the framing whose top 3 assumptions are most testable in 2 weeks.
- Write `<job>/picks/validate/02-PICK.md`.

## Guards

- No research and no verdict in this stage.
- Inputs that disagree become a question for the founder; never pick one silently.
- The one-liner promises nothing undecided.

## Checks after

- Every price, size, rule or rival is tagged "Guess, not tested".
- Founder-reported items tagged "as reported".
- The five fatal questions are all covered.
- Each test has pass, fail and inconclusive numbers set in advance, and tests money or time, never "I would use it".
- No filler assumptions; each risk kind has an item or "none, because ...".
- Red and yellow flags at the end of both files.
- Checks never rewrite the skill's questions or voice.

## Override

As in `../RULES-RESEARCH.md` section 7.

## Out

- `idea/IDEA.md`: 1 One line, 2 Type, 3 Who pays, 4 What it is not, 5 Search words, 6 Input conflicts.
- `idea/ASSUMPTIONS.md`: ranked, top 3 marked, status column.
- `idea/QUESTIONS.md`, only in the Validate way in (as in stage 1, aimed at the top 3 assumptions).
- `picks/validate/02-PICK.md`.

## Stop point

The founder OKs the frame before stage 3.
