# Stage 9 - First 100 users

Part B: runs only after "go" at the gate and the user's OK. Read `../RULES-RESEARCH.md` and `BRAIN.md` first. Paths are from `kinso-validate/`. Old rewrite: `references/rewrite/09-first-users.md`.

## Runs

1. `skills/go-to-market/SKILL.md` (emotix, the stage winner, with `skills/shared/conventions.md`): named communities, top channels with cost and first action, communities, partnerships, one product-specific tactic.
2. `skills/persona-gen/SKILL.md` (emotix): 3 clearly different personas; for B2B one technical and one economic buyer; workarounds with cost; fit score (urgency, willingness to pay, reachability).
3. `skills/beachhead-segment/SKILL.md`: first segment on burning pain, willingness to pay, winnable share, referrals.
4. `skills/startup-design/references/research-wave-3-customers.md` C3 (anti-persona, where to reach) and `research-wave-4-distribution.md` D1 (channel tiers, channels to avoid).
5. `skills/mom-test-outreach/SKILL.md`: framing for the first cold ask.
6. Optional rehearsal: `skills/mom-test-roleplay/SKILL.md`.

Skip: persona-gen identity fields (names, ages, incomes, day in the life): invented facts. go-to-market launch-day tactics and day 30/60/90 metrics (this is a pre-launch test). Launch platforms that do not fit the product.

## In

- `evidence/SIGNALS.md` and `signals.csv`, `market/RIVALS.md`, `market/REGION.md`, `GATE.md`, `START.md` (money and time).
- Shared rules: facts only, dated sources, free only, never a `-contributor` model, collector at the 30-second pace with `--out <job>/raw.jsonl`, privacy, user override. About 12 calls.

## Deal and pick

- Deal 3 evidence-built buyer profiles that must be clearly different (persona-gen, beachhead-segment, C3). Every trait points to a signal row. Fewer than 2 rows: "thin".
- Deal 3 first-test messages, each in the buyer's own checked words.
- Optional: rehearse the top message against a roleplay persona built from the evidence, difficulty 4. Never counted as evidence.
- Rubric lines (0 to 3), profiles: urgency, willingness to pay, reachability, each with its rows. Messages: uses buyer words, answers the top objection, asks for a dated commitment.
- Write `<job>/picks/validate/09-PICK.md`.

## Guards

- Never contact, post or message anyone. The skill plans; the user runs it.
- Never publish unchecked legal or safety content as a test.
- Never name private people.

## Checks after

- "Built from evidence, not interviews" line at the top.
- A counted list of targets (for example "135 clubs in the city directory").
- Each channel has a link, a named place, free or paid, a dated cost or [Guess], and a post within 90 days.
- Community self-promotion rule quoted with `fetch`, or "unread".
- Fixed number to contact, price fixed before the test, payment or pre-commitment as the pass, pass, fail and fallback set.
- Harder side of a marketplace first; non-region signals ignored.
- Checks never rewrite the skill's voice.
