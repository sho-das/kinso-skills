# Stage 1 - Brainstorm

Read `../RULES-RESEARCH.md` and `BRAIN.md` first. Paths are from `kinso-validate/`. Old rewrite: `references/rewrite/01-brainstorm.md`.

## Runs

1. `skills/product-brainstorming/SKILL.md` (Anthropic, the stage winner): its modes, framework library, and session rhythm (frame, diverge, provoke, converge, capture). At least 5 to 7 approaches before judging.
2. `skills/idea-refine/SKILL.md` with `frameworks.md` and `refinement-criteria.md`: 7 lenses, 5 to 8 variations each with a story, 2 to 3 directions, hidden assumptions, "Not Doing" list.
3. `skills/office-hours/SKILL.md` and `sections/phase-2a-startup-diagnostic.md`: forcing questions routed by product stage, pushback patterns, minimal / ideal / lateral alternatives, EUREKA check.
4. For `idea/QUESTIONS.md`: `skills/mom-test/SKILL.md`, then `skills/mom-test-check-questions/SKILL.md` on every question.
5. Optional rehearsal: `skills/mom-test-roleplay/SKILL.md` with its `personas/`.

Skip: office-hours preamble, telemetry and `skills/gstack-shared/bin/`; office-hours Codex steps; `skills/idea-refine/scripts/`. Do not use the "recommended answer" format for interview questions (it made leading questions in the stage 1 test).

## In

- The founder's words (voice note or text), `START.md`, and `explore/TOPICS.md` if stage 0 ran.
- Shared rules: facts only, free only, never a `-contributor` model, privacy, user override. No web.

## Deal and pick

- Seeded draw (CORE section 5) of 3 lens cards from idea-refine's 7 lenses, SCAMPER and product-brainstorming's techniques. At least one "expand" card (10x, constraint removal) and one "cut" card (inversion, eliminate, simplify). Each card gives 5 to 8 variations with a one-line story.
- The judge clusters them into 3 to 5 directions. The founder's idea stays direction 1. One no-build test stays. At least one direction is the "ideal" or "10x" shape.
- Optional: seeded draw of 1 mom-test persona at difficulty 3 to 5 (`random <industry> <difficulty>`). Run `QUESTIONS.md` against it. Rehearsal only, never evidence.
- Rubric lines (0 to 3): painkiller or vitamin; "can you name 3 real people"; fits `START.md` limits; one bold option present.
- Write `<job>/picks/validate/01-PICK.md`.

## Guards

- Founder's words first; never replace their idea with ours.
- No judging of demand here; that starts in stage 3.
- No invented numbers in any variation; "no count given".

## Checks after

- Quotes of the founder carry who said them.
- One problem per line; mixed problems split.
- Suggestions marked as suggestions.
- Legal points: "check with an official source".
- "I would pay" is a wish, not evidence.
- Every interview question passes the mom-test bad-question test; at most 5 questions for the founder.
- Checks never cut the number of directions or the ambition.

## Override

As in `../RULES-RESEARCH.md` section 7.

## Out

- `idea/BRAINSTORM.md`: 1 What you said, 2 The problems, 3 Questions for you, 4 Problem statements, 5 Directions and Not doing, 6 If you asked me to pick.
- `idea/QUESTIONS.md`.
- `picks/validate/01-PICK.md`.

## Stop point

The founder picks the problem and direction before stage 2.
