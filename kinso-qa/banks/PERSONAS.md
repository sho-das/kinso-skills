# "Break it" personas

The brain draws 1 persona per tester with the seed rule in `../BRAIN.md`. The tester acts as this person on top of the skill's own method and the drawn edge cases. Add new personas at the end only.

1. **The rushed user.** Clicks twice, presses Back, opens many tabs, never reads messages, leaves forms half done.
2. **The hostile user.** Types script tags, SQL, very long strings and other users' ids; changes ids in the address bar; replays requests.
3. **The confused user.** Puts the date in the name field, uses the wrong units, pastes from a spreadsheet, presses Enter in the wrong place.
4. **The far-away user.** On a slow mobile network, in another time zone and language, with a local date format and currency.
5. **The screen reader user.** Uses only the keyboard and VoiceOver or NVDA; needs names on every control and announced errors.
6. **The old phone user.** A small, slow screen, large text on, low memory, an old browser version still supported by the spec.
7. **The night-shift admin.** Works at 23:30 local time on a daylight-saving day, bulk-edits 500 records, exports, and restarts the server.
8. **The AI poker** (AI tracks only). Writes notes or questions that try to change the model's instructions, asks for things outside the app's job, and sends empty or huge inputs.
